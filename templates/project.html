Student Performance Prediction System

An AI-powered platform that predicts student mathematics scores from demographic and academic inputs, classifies performance levels, and provides interactive tutoring through a built-in LLM chatbot.

🔗

Prediction Pipeline

⚙️

How It Works

01
Data Collection

Raw student data — gender, ethnicity, parental education, lunch type, test prep, reading and writing scores — forms the feature set.

02
Preprocessing & Transformation

Missing values are handled, categorical features encoded, and numerical values scaled so every model trains on clean, normalised input.

03
Model Training & Selection

Seven regression algorithms are trained and ranked by R² score. The best model is serialised as a reusable artifact.

04
Real-Time Prediction

On form submission the artifact is loaded, input is transformed through the saved pipeline, and a maths score is predicted in under 0.1 s.

05
AI Tutor Interaction

Students can ask the floating chatbot any academic question; the Groq-backed LLM responds with step-by-step explanations in real time.

Performance Classification

🟢 No Assistance Needed

Score ≥ 60 — Student is performing well and typically needs no additional support.

🟡 Needs Support

Score 40 – 59 — Student may benefit from additional guidance and focused attention.

🔴 Needs Significant Help

Score < 40 — Student requires early intervention and strong academic assistance.
